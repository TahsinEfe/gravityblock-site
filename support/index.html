Frequently asked

How do I play GravityBlock?+

Choose one of the three pieces at the bottom and place it in an open space on the 8×8 board. Completed rows or columns clear. Swipe the board to move every block toward the selected edge.

Where are my progress and high scores stored?+

Your current game, high scores, and settings are stored on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data may also remove these records.

How can I turn off sound and haptics?+

Open Settings from the main menu. You can change sound and haptic feedback independently from there.

Can I reduce motion effects?+

Yes. GravityBlock respects your device’s Reduce Motion preference. Color symbols also help you tell blocks apart without relying on color alone.

What should I do if the game stops responding?+

First, close the app completely and reopen it. If the issue continues, include your device model, iOS version, GravityBlock version, and the steps that reproduce the problem in your support request.
